Flower Care Guide

Keep your handmade blooms looking pristine and elegant for as long as possible with these simple care tips:

  • Preserved & Dried Flowers: Keep away from direct sunlight, high humidity, and water. No watering is required! Simply dust gently with a soft brush when necessary.
  • Fresh Accent Blooms: If your arrangement includes fresh stems, keep them in a cool room away from direct heat sources and sunlight.
  • Handling: Handle your bouquet wrapping with care to preserve the original artisan structure and shape.
